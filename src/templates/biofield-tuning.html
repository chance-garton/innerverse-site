Meet Chance Garton

This work began with a change in my own life.

In late 2019, hearing Eileen Day McKusick speak about Biofield Tuning led me to try a tuning fork on a persistent shoulder injury. The change I experienced set me on a path of studying her work, learning the Biofield Anatomy, and practicing with others.

I have been working with clients since 2021. My path was self-directed, built through study, practice, and exchanges with certified practitioners. I also draw on years of exploring energy medicine with the guests of my podcast, InnerVerse. I bring that experience, curiosity, and personal attention to every session.

How I came to this work

Something inside me rang like a bell.

In late 2019, I came across a few podcasts featuring Eileen Day McKusick. I had never before, and have never since, felt so absolutely sure that a podcast had just given me a life-changing new direction to pursue.

At the time, I was trying to recover from a serious left shoulder injury. For nearly a year, I had lived with pain and limited mobility. I could not raise my arm above shoulder height. I attributed it to rock climbing, which I had been doing regularly for several years. I would take a week off, then two weeks, then a month, and try different ways to rest and recover. Nothing seemed to change.

After hearing Eileen, I bought her Sonic Slider tuning fork and began using it on my shoulder. I noticed less pain on the first day. In less than a week, my mobility returned. The injury never came back.

It took more time, and a deeper reading of Tuning the Human Biofield, for me to understand that experience in a different way. Through Eileen's Biofield Anatomy, I came to see a connection between the shoulder and an emotional pattern I had been living out.

What followed was a difficult period in which my first wife and I separated. Looking back, I recognized my own part in the unhealthy dynamics between us. I had a subconscious need to make a partner the bad guy, while seeing myself as the selfless person who cared for and supported them without appreciation. That narrative gave me someone to blame for why I was not growing, and someone against whom I could feel like the good guy.

The left shoulder's associations in the Biofield Anatomy closely matched that pattern. I eventually came to understand my recovery as more than a change in my shoulder: it was also an invitation to let go of a victim narrative. Recognizing that story, and my attachment to it, helped me take responsibility for moving beyond it.

I was so amazed by the transformation in my own life that I wanted to bring this work to others. I interviewed Eileen on InnerVerse, studied the Biofield Anatomy, and began practicing with friends and later with podcast listeners remotely. I also brought previous experience with intuitive energy work to the process.

By 2022, my practice had grown from experimental self-training into a substantial part of my livelihood. Since then, I have both worked on certified Biofield Tuning practitioners and received sessions from them. Those exchanges have helped me compare and refine my approach. My work also draws on other modalities I have explored through years of study and conversations with InnerVerse guests.

I encourage people interested in becoming practitioners to pursue official training. That was not my own path. I learned through study, practice, personal experience, and ongoing exchange with others, and I continue to approach this work as a full-time learner.

Straight Answers

Questions people ask before booking

What do I actually need for a remote session?

A quiet room where you will not be interrupted, a device that runs Zoom, and somewhere comfortable to sit or recline. Headphones are welcome but not required. You will be engaged and participating throughout the tuning, sharing what you notice as we work. Most people feel very relaxed during and afterward, while remaining awake and involved.

Does this conflict with my religion or belief system?

Biofield Tuning sessions are not inherently religious, and you are welcome to bring your own faith into a session.

I do not invoke spirits or other entities. My only invocation before a session is to the supreme being, the God of the Universe.

What will I feel during a session?

In my experience, everyone feels something, though what they feel differs from person to person. You may notice warmth, tingling, waves of emotion, or a deep sense of relaxation. There is no wrong way to receive a tuning, and no particular sensation you need to aim for. Your experience is the way it is meant to be for you.

How soon will I notice a difference?

Most people report feeling lighter by the end of the session itself. In my experience, it typically takes about three days for the body to catch up with shifts in the energy field, while integration can continue to unfold for weeks.

How many sessions will I need?

In my experience, many people find that one session either resolves the issue they came with or helps them see a path forward. Bodies are like instruments: both can benefit from a periodic tune up. Some of my long-term clients return every three, six, or twelve months. It varies by individual.

Pay attention to your progress and your sense of momentum in life and health. If growth is good and consistent, keep going. If that momentum fades or progress begins to reverse, that can be a useful time to consider another session.

I generally leave at least a month between tuning sessions for the same person. Time to integrate changes, develop new habits, and unpack further insights is part of the process.

If you would like to plan ahead, the three-session bundle is $500, saving $100. Sessions never expire and can also be gifted to other people. Buying a bundle does not mean you need to book all three close together.

Is this a substitute for medical care?

Biofield Tuning is a different category of work from medical care, not a replacement for it. Nothing in a session constitutes diagnosis or treatment of a medical condition. There are also situations where tuning is not advised, which are listed in full at the bottom of this page.

What are your cancellation, rescheduling, and refund policies?

Rescheduling: There is no penalty for rescheduling through the calendar invite generated by your booking, or for emailing me to arrange a different time.

Missed appointments: If you miss your appointment without giving at least 30 minutes' notice, there is a $25 fee to reschedule.

Prepaid sessions: Unused sessions from a three-session bundle never expire and may be gifted to someone else.

If you are dissatisfied: Please contact me. I handle concerns individually and will work with you toward a fair resolution, which may include another session or a refund.
